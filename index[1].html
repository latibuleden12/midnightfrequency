02 / ABOUT THE STATION

A little comfort.
A little chaos.

Midnight Frequency is a fictional late-night radio station for the sleepless, the curious, and anyone who has ever felt that the dark was listening back.

Expect strange stories, cozy corners, questionable advertisements, unexpected callers, and a host doing her best to keep the signal steady.

Keep your dial right here. — The Host
